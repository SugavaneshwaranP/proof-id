# Landing Page & Content Specification

> **Document Path**: `docs/UI_CONTENT_SPECIFICATION.md`  
> **Brand Name**: Backed (Proof-Backed Identity Network)

---

## 🎯 Core Landing Page Purpose
Explain what **Backed** is and guide first-time visitors to create a page backed by real proof.

---

## 🧱 Component Layout Breakdown

### 1. Navigation Bar (NAV)
- **Logo**: `Backed.`
- **Nav Links**: How it works · For Business · Log in
- **Primary Button**: `Create your page`

---

### 2. Hero Section
- **Hero Headline**: 
  > *"Prove it. Don’t just say it."*
- **Hero Subhead**:
  > *"Backed turns your skills, your work and your credentials into one page people actually trust — because it’s backed by real proof, not just words."*
- **Hero Action Buttons**:
  - `Create your free page`
  - `See an example`
- **Interactive Visual**: Tactile proof card file visualizer with binder clips, stamp badges (`VERIFIED PROOF`), and real proof items.

---

### 3. How It Works (3-Step Strip)
- **Step 1**: `1. Add what you do` — List your skills, or what your business offers, in plain language.
- **Step 2**: `2. Back it up` — Attach a real project, a certificate, or a work sample as proof.
- **Step 3**: `3. Share one link` — Send a single page that shows what you can do, and proves it.

---

### 4. Person / Business Split Cards
- **Person Card**:
  - *For individuals — Turn your experience into a page that speaks for itself.*
  - **Button**: `Build my page`
- **Business Card**:
  - *For businesses — Show buyers what you make, how much, and how fast — with the proof to match.*
  - **Button**: `List my business`

---

### 5. Proof Examples Section
- Interactive cards featuring real proof samples (GitHub PRs, ISO 9001 certificates, AWS credentials, 5-axis CNC tolerance reports) with 1-click modal inspection.

---

### 6. Trust Strip
- **Trust Headline**: 
  > *"Every claim can be backed. Every proof can be checked."*
- **Trust Principles**: 100% Deterministic Engine · Zero AI Hallucination Rate · Row Level Security (RLS) · Short-Lived Signed Storage URLs.

---

### 7. Closing CTA Band
- **Closing Headline**:
  > *"Your work speaks. Let it be heard."*
- **Primary Button**: `Get started — it’s free`

---

### 8. Footer
- **Navigation Groups**: Product · Company · Legal
- **Copyright Statement**: `© 2026 Backed. Built on real proof`

---

### 9. Log In Page (`/login`)
- **Purpose**: Return a known user to their dashboard.
- **Headline**: *“Welcome back”*
- **Subhead**: *Log in to manage your page.*
- **Inputs**: Email address (`you@email.com`), Password
- **Primary Action**: `Log in` button
- **Links**: `Forgot your password?`
- **OAuth Integration**: `Continue with Google` (with divider `or`)
- **Footer Link**: `New here? Create your page`
